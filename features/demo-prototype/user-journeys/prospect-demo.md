# Prospect watches the demo

## Actors

- **Rare Kaon lead:** drives the demo on a call or in a meeting.
- **Prospect:** an owner, operator or private equity principal who already knows the tool
  they want.

## Before the demo

The lead knows the prospect's tool, in the prospect's words, and where the prospect's work
is recorded: mail, shared drives, a line-of-business system.

## Flow

| # | Lead does | Prospect sees | Prospect takes away |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Names the prospect's tool. Introduces Rivermark: a synthetic building services firm whose records were made for the demo. | The Rivermark records: files by source and kind, the period they cover | The method is shown on made-up records, not presented as a customer result |
| 1 | Starts from the stock schema for field service. | The fixed layer (ask, agree, do, accept) and stock categories | Chamber does not start from a blank page |
| 2 | Shows one refinement round and an expert confirming a category. | Categories in Rivermark's own words, coverage and thin categories, and the expert's decision | The business's people shape the schema. The machine does not decide alone. |
| 3 | Shows the records read into the schema. | What was read, what was skipped and why, and what is low-confidence | Chamber states its gaps |
| 4 | Opens one exposure, then the process map, then the life of one job with an exception. Every number opens its source sentences. | One risk the owner would act on, where work waits, one job's full history | The records show how the work runs, exceptions included, and every claim traces to a sentence |
| 5 | Opens the specification for the tool Rivermark wants. | Requirements, each with its evidence and acceptance criteria. One exception that interviews would miss. | The specification comes from the records, not from memory |
| 6 | Replays past cases against the build. | Cases that pass, one that fails, and what the business did in each | The build is checked against what really happened before a user sees it |
| 7 | Traces the failed case to a wrong term in the map, fixes it, and shows map version 2 against version 1. | The change and its effect on the case | When the business changes, the map and the software keep up |
| 8 | Asks for one tool, one owner and an agreed set of records. | — | A first engagement is small and concrete |

## Questions the lead must answer plainly

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Who sees our records, and where does the text go? | Rare Kaon reads only the sources the customer approves, in a deployment for that customer. The data terms for model APIs are not yet written. Do not claim them. |
| Our business runs on one person in the back office. | The map shows who holds the exceptions. Rare Kaon spends focused time with those people. |
| Some of our work is on paper or by phone. | That work is outside what Chamber reads. Where it matters, Rare Kaon captures it another way. |
| Is this a real customer? | No. Rivermark is synthetic. No customer result is demonstrated yet. |
| How long does it take, and what does it cost? | Not claimed in the demo. |

## Success

The prospect names their own tool and the records it depends on, and asks what a first
engagement needs from them.
